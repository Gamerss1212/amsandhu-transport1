AI CLIPPER - WINDOWS

SETUP (once)
1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents\AIClipper.
   (Right-click the zip -> Extract All. Don't run it from inside the zip.)
2. Double-click AIClipper.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
   (This appears because the app isn't code-signed.)
3. A black window opens, then your browser opens AI Clipper.
   Keep the black window open while you use it - closing it quits the app.
   No keys or accounts are needed - it's free.

MAKE CLIPS
4. Choose where the videos come from:
     - "Find videos for me": it studies 350+ short videos to learn what's going viral right now,
       finds popular podcasts/interviews (10 minutes to 200 hours long) from the last 2 weeks,
       and watches them in full.
     - "Use my videos": paste YouTube links or video file paths, one per line.
       Any length from 10 minutes up to 200 hours works. Over 3 hours, only the audio is
       downloaded to find the moments, then just the chosen clips are downloaded in full quality.
       Tip: in File Explorer, Shift + right-click a video -> "Copy as path", then paste.
5. Choose how many clips you want (1 to 100). They are made in layers of 5, so the first
   clips show up early, and they can come from different videos - only the best make it.
6. Press the big button. The AI picks the edit each clip needs (fast cuts, shake, sound effects
   for hype moments; calm captions and music for heartfelt ones; a stacked two-speaker view for
   podcast two-shots). The first run takes longer (it downloads the speech model, ~500 MB, once).
   A 1-hour video takes roughly 10-40 minutes depending on your PC.

YOUR CLIPS
- They appear in the app with Play / Download / Copy caption buttons, and are saved in
  the "output" folder next to AIClipper.exe (video + thumbnail + ready-to-post caption +
  .srt subtitles).
- Don't like one? Press the X on it. It's deleted, and the app learns to find different clips.

IF YOUTUBE SAYS "CONFIRM YOU'RE NOT A BOT"
YouTube sometimes stops downloads from a connection until it sees you're signed in. Fix it once:
- Easiest: open Firefox, sign in at youtube.com, then press the button in AI Clipper again.
- Chrome/Edge: click "Fix YouTube access" in the app (or "YouTube login" at the top) and follow the
  3 steps to upload your cookies.txt (free "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" extension -> Export).
- Or choose "Use my videos" and paste video files you already downloaded - no YouTube needed.

OTHER FILES (next to AIClipper.exe)
  config.yaml   settings: channels to watch, search topics, strictness, clip length
  .env          optional extra keys (not needed)
  assets\       optional music, sound effects, fonts - and gameplay/b-roll videos in assets\broll
                to turn on the split-screen layout

CHECK YOUR SETUP
  Open a Command Prompt in this folder and run:  AIClipper.exe doctor
