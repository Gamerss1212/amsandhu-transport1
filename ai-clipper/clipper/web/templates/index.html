      Fix YouTube access

      YouTube sometimes stops downloads from a connection until it sees you're signed in. Pick one - you only do this once.
      Easiest - with Firefox
      1. Open Firefox, go to youtube.com and sign in.
      2. Come back here and press Get clips again. AI Clipper uses that login automatically.
      With Chrome or Edge
      1. Add the free extension "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" to your browser.
      2. Open youtube.com (signed in), click the extension and choose Export.
      3. Drop the downloaded file here:
      Your login stays on this PC (data\youtube_cookies.txt) and is only used to download from YouTube. Don't share that file with anyone.
